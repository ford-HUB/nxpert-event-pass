# Event Registration — Participant Guide

For attendees registering for events and using their QR entry pass.
(Organizers have a separate guide: **ADMIN-GUIDE.md**.)

> You do **not** need an account to attend a public event. Having one lets you **browse all
> open events**, keeps your passes together under **My Events**, and shows your photo on the
> welcome screen at check-in.

> **How registration works:** when you register you are **not** issued a pass immediately.
> Your registration is **pending** until an organizer **confirms** it. Once confirmed, your
> **QR entry pass is emailed to you** (and, if you have an account, it becomes available under
> **My Events**). Only a confirmed pass can be scanned at the door.

---

## 1. Browsing events (account holders)

Sign in and open the **Events** menu to see **all events open for registration**, shown as
cards with the event banner, dates, venue, and a short description.

- Use the **search** box to find an event by title, venue, or description.
- Click **View details** on a card to open a pop-up with the full information: the **banner**,
  **registration fees**, the **schedule of activities**, and any **sub-events**.
- Click **Register** to open the event's registration page and sign up.

---

## 2. Registering for an event

You reach the registration page from **Events → Register**, from a shared public link, or
from an **invitation email** (click *Register now*). The page shows the event details,
banner, **registration fees**, and **schedule of activities**.

- **Signed in:** your **name** and **Senate No.** are shown from your account (read-only).
- **Not signed in (guest):** a short form appears — enter your **name**; email and phone are
  optional (add an **email** so your pass can be emailed once confirmed).

### Choosing sub-events

If the event runs over several days or sessions, you'll see a **Choose sub-events** list
grouped by day. **Tick each sub-event you want to attend** and submit once — you'll get a
**separate registration (and, once confirmed, a separate pass) for each**. Full sub-events
can't be selected.

Answer any **additional questions** (required ones are marked with a red asterisk *), then
click **Register**. You'll see a **"Registration received — pending confirmation"** message.

> If the event or a sub-event is **full** or **closed**, you'll see the reason instead of the
> form.

---

## 3. Your entry pass (QR code)

You get your **QR entry pass by email once an organizer confirms your registration** — there
is no pass on the registration screen anymore. Account holders can also open it any time from
**My Events → Show pass** after it's confirmed.

On event day, show the QR — on your phone or printed — to the organizer's scanner.

---

## 4. Creating an account / signing in

From the sign-in page choose **Create a participant account** and provide:

- **First name**, **M.I.** (optional), and **Last name**
- **Senate No.** — **required**
- **Level** — **required**; choose from the list (e.g. *Senator*, *Chairman Circle*, …)
- **Email** and **password**; optionally **phone** and **organization**
- **Profile photo** (optional) — click **Add photo**. Your photo is shown on the big
  **welcome screen** when you check in at an event.

Your name and Senate No. are then filled in automatically (read-only) whenever you register
while signed in. If the organizer enabled **email verification**, click the link in the
confirmation email before registering (a banner reminds you and can **resend** it).

---

## 5. My Events (account holders)

Everything you're registered for, shown as cards. Each card shows the event (and the
**sub-event** name, if applicable) and a **status**:

- 🟠 **Pending confirmation** — waiting for the organizer to confirm. Your pass isn't
  available yet.
- 🟢 **Confirmed** — approved; your pass is ready.
- 🟢 **Checked in** — you've attended.

On each card:

- **Show pass** — opens your QR pass (available once **confirmed**).
- **Rate** — leave a 1–5 star rating and optional comment.
- **⋮ menu:**
  - **Re-send pass email** — emails your pass again (available once confirmed).
  - **Cancel registration** — removes your spot (disabled once you're checked in).

---

## 6. On event day

1. Have your **confirmed** QR pass ready — from the email or **My Events → Show pass**.
2. Present it to the organizer's scanner.
3. A green **"Welcome!"** with your name (and your photo, if you added one) means you're
   checked in. Done!

---

## Appendix — Participant quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| I registered but didn't get a pass | Passes are sent **after an organizer confirms** your registration. You'll get it by email once confirmed; account holders can also use **My Events → Show pass**. |
| My registration says **Pending confirmation** | Normal — an organizer needs to confirm it. The pass arrives once they do. |
| Where do I see all events I can join? | Sign in and open the **Events** menu; click **View details** for fees, schedule, and sub-events. |
| How do I register for a multi-day event? | On the registration page, **tick the sub-events** you want and submit once — you get a separate pass for each. |
| I lost my QR pass | Re-open the emailed pass, or (with an account) **My Events → Show pass** / **Re-send pass email** — once it's confirmed. |
| The event / sub-event says **full** or **closed** | Capacity is reached or registration is closed — contact the organizer. |
| Do I need an account to attend? | No for public events. An account lets you browse events, keeps passes together, and shows your photo at check-in. |
| Can I register twice? | No — re-registering with the same details won't create duplicates. |
| Can I cancel? | Yes, from **My Events → ⋮ → Cancel registration**, unless you're already checked in. |
| Do I have to answer every extra question? | Only the ones marked with a red asterisk (*). |
| Why can't I edit my name / Senate No. on the form? | They come from your account and are read-only. |
| What is "Level"? | Your membership level, chosen at sign-up; it's shown on the welcome screen at check-in. |
| Will my photo be used? | Only to greet you on the check-in welcome screen. It's optional. |
