# NXPass — Participant Guide

For attendees registering for events and using their entry pass or online meeting link.
(Organizers have a separate guide: **ADMIN-GUIDE.md**.)

> You do **not** need an account to attend a public event. An account lets you **browse all open
> events**, keeps your passes together under **My Events**, and shows your photo on the welcome
> screen at check-in.

> **How registration works:** when you register your registration is **pending** — no pass is
> issued yet. Once an organizer **confirms** it, your **QR entry pass** is sent to you by your
> chosen channel (email and/or Viber). For **online** events you receive the **meeting link and a
> calendar invite** instead. Only a confirmed pass can be scanned at the door.

---

## 1. Signing in / creating an account

**Sign in** with your **username or email** and password (the eye icon shows the password).

To create an account, choose **Create a participant account** and fill in:

- **First name**, **M.I.** (optional), **Last name**
- **ID No.** — **required**
- **Designation** — **required**; type your designation or title
- **Email** and **password** (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number)
- **Phone** (optional) and **chapter**
- **Profile photo** (optional) — **Add photo**; shown on the welcome screen when you check in

If the organizer turned on **email verification**, click the link in the verification email
before registering for events (a banner reminds you and has **Resend email**).

### The Home screen

After signing in you see **Home** with cards for **Browse Events**, **My Events** and **My
Account**. The **Home** button at the top-left always brings you back. The **bell** shows your
notifications (for example when a registration is approved), and the menu under your **name**
has **Edit account**, **Install app** and **Sign out**.

> **Install the app:** on a phone, tap **Install** on the banner (on iPhone: **Share → Add to
> Home Screen**) to open NXPass like an app.

---

## 2. Browsing events (account holders)

**Browse Events** shows every event open for registration as a card: the banner, date, a status
tag (**Open**, **Full**, **Ended**, **Registered**), an **Online** / **Hybrid** tag when the
event is held online, the time and venue, and the start of the description.

- Use the **search** box to find an event by title, venue or description.
- **Details** opens the full information: banner, description, registration fees, schedule of
  activities, sub-events and meal stubs.
- **Register** opens the registration page (**Add sub-event** if you're already registered for
  part of it; **Registered** if you have everything).

---

## 3. Registering for an event

You reach the registration page from **Browse Events → Register**, a shared public link, or an
**invitation email** (*Register now*). The page shows the event's date and time, where it takes
place (venue, **Online meeting**, or both), the **description exactly as the organizer wrote
it**, fees, schedule and meal stubs.

- **Signed in:** your **name** and **ID No.** come from your account (read-only) — click
  **Register for this event**.
- **Not signed in (guest):** fill in
  - **Full name** and **ID No.**
  - **Send my pass via** — Email, Viber, or Both
  - **Email** and **Mobile number** (+63 9XX XXX XXXX) — both required
  - **Display picture** (optional) — shown to staff at check-in
  - **Face check-in** (optional) — tick the consent box, then **Add my face** to be checked in
    by face recognition

### Sub-events

If the event has several days or sessions, tick each **sub-event** you want. Each one is a
**separate registration** with its own pass, confirmed separately. Full sub-events can't be
picked.

Answer any **additional questions** (required ones have a red *; some appear only after you
answer an earlier question; some ask you to **Upload image**). Then submit — you'll see
**"Registration received!"** with a pending-confirmation note.

> If the event or a sub-event is **full** or **closed**, you'll see **Registration unavailable**
> instead of the form. With no internet, your registration is saved on the device and sent
> automatically when you're back online.

---

## 4. Your pass or meeting link

**In-person events:** once confirmed, your **QR entry pass** is sent to you (email, Viber, or
both). Account holders can also open it any time from **My Events → Show pass**. Meal stubs, if
the event has them, arrive with the pass — present each at the meal counter.

**Online events:** once confirmed, you receive an email with a **Join online meeting** button and
a **calendar invite** — open the invite to add the meeting to your calendar (Outlook, Gmail or
your phone). There's no QR pass for online-only events. Account holders also get a **Join
meeting** button in **My Events**.

**Hybrid events:** you get both the QR pass (for attending in person) and the meeting link.

> If the organizer changes the meeting time or link, you'll receive an **updated invite** that
> replaces the old calendar entry automatically. If the event is cancelled, the meeting is
> removed from your calendar.

---

## 5. My Events (account holders)

Every registration is a card with the date, event (and sub-event) name, where it takes place, and
a **status**:

- 🟠 **Pending confirmation** — waiting for the organizer; your pass/link isn't available yet.
- 🟢 **Confirmed** — approved; your pass / meeting link is ready.
- 🟢 **Checked in** — you've attended.

On each card:

- **Join meeting** — online / hybrid events, once confirmed.
- **Show pass** — your QR pass (in-person / hybrid events, once confirmed).
- **⋮ menu:**
  - **Edit my responses** — change your answers to the event's questions.
  - **Re-send pass email** — once confirmed.
  - **Cancel registration** — asks you to confirm; not possible once you're checked in. For an
    online event, the meeting is also removed from your calendar.

---

## 6. Editing your answers

You can change your answers to an event's questions until you check in or the event ends:

- **Account holders:** **My Events → ⋮ → Edit my responses**.
- **Anyone:** use the **Edit your response** link in your emails, or on the event's registration
  page choose **Already registered? Edit your response** and either
  - enter your **ID No.** and **registered name** → **Open my response**, or
  - enter your **email or mobile number** → **Send my edit link**.

---

## 7. My Account

**My Account** lets you update your **profile photo**, **face recognition** (**Register face** /
**Re-capture face** / **Remove face**), **name**, **ID No.**, **Designation**, **chapter**,
**Send my pass via** (Email or Viber — a phone is needed for Viber), and **change your password**
(current password required). To change your email, contact an administrator.

---

## 8. On event day

1. Have your **confirmed** QR pass ready — from the email/Viber message or **My Events → Show
   pass** — or simply walk up to the camera if you registered your face.
2. Present your pass to the scanner (or look straight at the camera for face check-in, close
   enough for your face to be clear — it takes a second or two).
3. A green **"Welcome!"** with your name (and photo, if you added one) means you're checked in.

For an **online** event, just click **Join online meeting** in your email or **Join meeting** in
*My Events* at the start time.

## Appendix — Participant quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| I registered but didn't get a pass | Passes are sent **after an organizer confirms** your registration. |
| My registration says **Pending confirmation** | Normal — the organizer still needs to confirm it. |
| Where's the link for an online event? | In your confirmation email (**Join online meeting**) and in **My Events → Join meeting**, once confirmed. It isn't shown before confirmation. |
| How do I add an online event to my calendar? | Open the **invite** attached to your confirmation email. Changes by the organizer update it automatically. |
| Where do I see all events I can join? | Sign in and open **Browse Events**; **Details** shows fees, schedule and sub-events. |
| How do I register for a multi-day event? | Tick the **sub-events** you want and submit once — you get a separate pass for each. |
| I lost my QR pass | Re-open the pass message, or **My Events → Show pass / Re-send pass email** (once confirmed). |
| I need to change my answers | **My Events → ⋮ → Edit my responses**, or **Edit your response** on the event page. |
| The event says **full** or **closed** | Capacity is reached or registration has closed — contact the organizer. |
| Do I need an account to attend? | No, not for public events. |
| Can I cancel? | Yes — **My Events → ⋮ → Cancel registration**, unless you're already checked in. |
| Why can't I edit my name / ID No. on the form? | They come from your account (read-only). Change them in **My Account**. |
| What is "Designation"? | Your title or designation, entered at sign-up; shown on the welcome screen at check-in. |
| Will my photo or face be used? | Only to greet you on the check-in screen and (if you registered your face) to check you in. Both are optional. |
